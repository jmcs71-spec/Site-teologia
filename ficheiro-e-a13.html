Eternal Life in Christ according to the Holy Bible

In the Bible, the expression eternal life refers to a life that never ends and is lived in communion with God. It does not simply mean living forever, but living forever with God in a restored relationship with Him.

According to the Bible, this eternal life is made possible through Jesus Christ.

1. What eternal life means

The Bible explains that eternal life is not only endless existence, but a life of relationship with God.

"And this is eternal life: that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent." (John 17:3)

Thus, eternal life involves knowing God and living in communion with Him.

2. Eternal life is a gift from God

The Bible teaches that eternal life is not something human beings can earn by their own merits. It is a gift offered by God.

"The gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord." (Romans 6:23)

3. Jesus Christ is the source of eternal life

According to the Bible, eternal life is directly connected to the person of Jesus Christ.

"I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live, even though they die." (John 11:25)
"God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son." (1 John 5:11)

4. When eternal life begins

Many people think eternal life only begins after death. However, the Bible teaches that it begins at the moment a person believes in Christ.

"Whoever hears my word and believes him who sent me has eternal life." (John 5:24)

This means eternal life starts now and continues forever.

5. The future hope of eternal life

The Bible also speaks of a future time when God will fully restore creation and remove suffering.

"He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain." (Revelation 21:4)

In this final state, believers will live forever with God.

Conclusion

According to the Bible, eternal life is living forever in communion with God. It is a gift offered by God’s grace through Jesus Christ. It begins at the moment of faith and culminates in an eternal existence with God, free from suffering.
