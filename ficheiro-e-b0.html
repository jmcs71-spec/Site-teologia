What Is Systematic Theology According to the Holy Bible

Systematic theology is an organized way of studying what the Bible teaches about God and everything related to Him.

Simply put: systematic theology is organizing the teachings of the Bible by topics in order to understand them better.

1. What “Theology” Means

The word “theology” means the study of God.

"Let us know; let us press on to know the Lord." (Hosea 6:3)

2. Why Study the Bible in an Organized Way

The Bible was written over many years and contains many themes. Systematic theology helps gather together everything the Bible says about a specific subject.

"Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved… rightly handling the word of truth." (2 Timothy 2:15)

3. The Bible as the Foundation of Theology

Systematic theology organizes what is already in the Bible.

"All Scripture is given by inspiration of God and is profitable for teaching." (2 Timothy 3:16)

4. Organizing by Topics

Systematic theology groups the teachings of the Bible into areas such as God, Jesus Christ, the Holy Spirit, humanity, sin, salvation, the church, and the end times.

5. The Purpose of Systematic Theology

The purpose is to know God better and live according to His will.

"You shall love the Lord your God with all your understanding." (Luke 10:27)

6. The Importance for Life

Systematic theology helps us better understand the Bible, avoid errors, and live with understanding.

"My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge." (Hosea 4:6)

Conclusion

According to the Bible, systematic theology organizes biblical teachings, is based on Scripture, and helps people know God and live rightly.
