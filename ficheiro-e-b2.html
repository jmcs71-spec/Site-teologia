Does God Exist? According to the Holy Bible

The Bible does not begin by trying to prove the existence of God as a philosophical argument. It assumes that God exists and presents signs of this reality in creation, in human conscience, and in history.

Simply put: the Bible states that God exists and that He reveals Himself to humanity.

1. The Bible Affirms That God Exists

Right at the beginning, God is presented as the fundamental reality.

"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth." (Genesis 1:1)

2. Creation Points to God

The world around us is seen as evidence of God's existence.

"The heavens declare the glory of God." (Psalm 19:1)
"His invisible attributes… are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made." (Romans 1:20)

3. Human Conscience Points to God

Human beings possess moral awareness and a search for meaning.

"They show the work of the law written in their hearts." (Romans 2:15)
"God has set eternity in the human heart." (Ecclesiastes 3:11)

4. God Reveals Himself to Humanity

God is not distant, but reveals Himself in various ways.

"He is not far from each one of us." (Acts 17:27)
"He who has seen Me has seen the Father." (John 14:9)

5. The Denial of God

The Bible states that denying God is ignoring what has already been revealed.

"The fool says in his heart, 'There is no God.'" (Psalm 14:1)

Conclusion

According to the Bible, God exists, is the Creator, and has left clear signs of His existence in creation and in humanity.

He invites every person to know Him.

Text based on the Holy Bible.
