Our Corruption According to the Holy Bible

Photo by Jesus Monroy Lazcano on Unsplash

The Bible teaches that human corruption is not merely the result of isolated mistakes or occasional moral failures. It describes a deep human condition: an inner tendency to turn away from God, goodness, and truth.

Simply put, the Bible says something within humanity has been broken.

1. Humanity Was Created Good, but Fell

According to the Bible, human beings were created by God with dignity, purpose, and goodness. However, at the beginning of human history, there was a conscious act of disobedience to God, known as the Fall.

“And God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very good.” (Genesis 1:31)
“Sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin.” (Romans 5:12)

2. What the Bible Means by Sin

In the Bible, sin is not merely doing bad things. Sin is living apart from God's will, placing the human self at the center of life.

“We all, like sheep, have gone astray; each of us has turned to our own way.” (Isaiah 53:6)

3. Corruption Is Universal

The Bible teaches that this condition affects all humanity, without exception. It is not only about people considered evil, but all human beings.

“There is none righteous, not even one.” (Romans 3:10)
“For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.” (Romans 3:23)

4. Corruption Comes from Within

According to the Bible, the main problem is not merely outward behavior, but the inner human heart.

“The heart is deceitful above all things and desperately wicked.” (Jeremiah 17:9)
“Out of the heart of men come evil thoughts.” (Mark 7:21)

5. The Consequences of Corruption

Human corruption has serious consequences, including separation from God, spiritual death, and moral disorder affecting both personal life and society.

“Your iniquities have separated you from your God.” (Isaiah 59:2)
“The wages of sin is death.” (Romans 6:23)

6. Humanity Cannot Heal Itself

The Bible teaches that humanity, by itself, cannot remove this corruption. Good intentions, laws, or religion are not enough to solve the problem at its root.

“Can the Ethiopian change his skin or the leopard its spots?” (Jeremiah 13:23)

7. God's Answer to Human Corruption

Despite this serious diagnosis, the Bible presents a solution based on God's love and grace. God acts to restore what humanity cannot restore by itself.

“But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.” (Romans 5:8)
“He was pierced for our transgressions.” (Isaiah 53:5)

Conclusion

According to the Bible, humanity was created good but became corrupted by sin. This corruption is universal, inward, and causes separation from God. Yet the biblical message does not end in condemnation, but in the hope of restoration offered by God.
