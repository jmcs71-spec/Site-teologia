What Happens After Death According to the Holy Bible

The Bible teaches that death is not the end of existence, but a transition into a spiritual and eternal reality.

Simply put: after death, every person stands before God and enters an eternal reality.

1. Death Is Not the End

Human beings continue to exist after death.

"It is appointed for men to die once, but after this the judgment." (Hebrews 9:27)

2. There Is a Judgment

All people will give an account to God.

"We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ." (2 Corinthians 5:10)

3. Two Eternal Destinies

The Bible speaks of two final destinies.

"These will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life." (Matthew 25:46)

4. Eternal Life with God

For those reconciled with God, there is a future of peace and joy.

"And so we will be with the Lord forever." (1 Thessalonians 4:17)
"God will wipe away every tear from their eyes." (Revelation 21:4)

5. Separation from God

The Bible also speaks of separation from God.

"They will suffer the punishment of eternal destruction, away from the presence of the Lord." (2 Thessalonians 1:9)

6. The Importance of Decisions in This Life

Eternal destiny is linked to the choices made in this life.

"Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life." (John 3:36)

Conclusion

According to the Bible, death is not the end, there is a judgment, and there are two eternal destinies: life with God or separation from God.

Present life has eternal consequences.

Text based on the Holy Bible.
