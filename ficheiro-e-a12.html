Our Salvation According to the Holy Bible

In the Bible, salvation means being delivered from the separation between human beings and God. This separation exists because of sin, but God has provided a way to restore that relationship.

Simply put, salvation is God’s act of rescuing human beings and giving them eternal life.

1. The Problem: Sin Separates Human Beings from God

According to the Bible, all human beings have failed morally and spiritually before God.

"For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God." (Romans 3:23)

Sin creates a separation between God and human beings.

"Your iniquities have separated you from your God." (Isaiah 59:2)

2. God's Initiative

The Bible teaches that salvation begins with God, not with human effort. God took the initiative to offer reconciliation.

"But God demonstrates His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us." (Romans 5:8)

3. The Role of Jesus Christ

According to the Bible, Jesus Christ is at the center of salvation. He lived without sin, died on the cross, and rose again, offering forgiveness and reconciliation with God.

"For there is one God and one mediator between God and mankind, the man Christ Jesus." (1 Timothy 2:5)
"I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me." (John 14:6)

4. Salvation Is a Gift from God

The Bible teaches that salvation is not earned through good works, religion, or personal effort. It is a gift offered by God.

"For by grace you have been saved through faith... it is the gift of God, not of works." (Ephesians 2:8-9)

5. How to Receive Salvation

The Bible teaches that salvation is received through faith in Jesus Christ.

"If you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved." (Romans 10:9)

This involves recognizing one’s own sin, trusting in Jesus as Savior, and accepting God's grace.

6. The Result of Salvation

According to the Bible, salvation brings several important results.

  • forgiveness of sins
  • reconciliation with God
  • new spiritual life
  • hope of eternal life
"If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation." (2 Corinthians 5:17)
"The free gift of God is eternal life." (Romans 6:23)

Conclusion

According to the Bible, all human beings need salvation. God took the initiative to provide that salvation through Jesus Christ. Salvation is received by God's grace through faith, and offers forgiveness, new life, and eternal hope.
