How the Holy Bible Was Formed

A clear and accessible explanation of the historical process through which the Bible was formed.

1. Lived Experiences Before the Texts

Before any books existed, there were real experiences of people and communities who believed they had encountered God. These events were first passed down orally.

2. From Oral Tradition to Writing

Over time, stories, laws, prayers, and teachings began to be written down so they could be preserved and passed on to future generations.

3. Many Authors and Styles

The Bible was written by many different authors, in different periods and historical settings, which explains the variety of literary styles found in the text.

4. The Selection of the Books (Canon)

Not every ancient religious text was included in the Bible. Religious communities recognized as sacred those writings they considered authentic, consistent, and widely used.

5. Old and New Testaments

  • Old Testament: texts written before Jesus, connected to the history and faith of Israel.
  • New Testament: texts about the life of Jesus and the beginning of Christianity.

6. Copying and Preservation

For centuries, biblical texts were copied by hand with great care. The existence of many manuscripts allows scholars to confirm the faithfulness of the text over time.

7. Translations

Originally written in Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek, the biblical texts have been translated into hundreds of languages, making the Bible accessible to people around the world.

Conclusion

The Bible was formed gradually throughout history through human experiences, oral tradition, writing, selection, and preservation. For Christians, this process was human and at the same time guided by God.
