About the Author of This Website

My name is José Simões, I am Portuguese and I became a Christian in August 2015. For a long time, I used to wonder about the reason for my existence in this world. Until the age of 15, I was Catholic due to my mother’s influence. When she passed away (I was 15 years old – I was born in 1971), I became skeptical – I was never an atheist.

After reading five books about the intelligence of Christ in 2015 by the psychiatrist Augusto Cury (from Brazil), I concluded that Jesus Christ truly existed. This author analyzed the intelligence of Christ without entering the field of faith. From that moment on, I became a Christian and started studying the Holy Bible.

Over time, I became a student at an online Brazilian school of Theology and gradually improved my knowledge about God.

My goal with this website is to help people better understand God (the true God) and to develop a good relationship with Him. This is the mission of a true Christian.

If anyone would like to gain more knowledge, they can contact me at the email: jmcs0871@gmail.com and I can guide them on how to access appropriate information about Theology.

If the visitor of this website speaks Portuguese, I can recommend suitable courses from the online school I attend at affordable prices (I became a student of this school in the summer of 2023). If they speak English, I can recommend an American church that I have been following online for several years.
