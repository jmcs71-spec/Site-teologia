Why Do We Exist According to the Holy Bible

The question “why do we exist?” is one of the deepest questions of human life. The Bible states that our existence is not an accident, but the result of an intentional decision by God.

Simply put: we exist because God chose to create us, with purpose and meaning.

1. We Were Created by God

The Bible teaches that God is the Creator of all things, including humanity.

"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth." (Genesis 1:1)
"God created man in His own image." (Genesis 1:27)

2. We Were Created in the Image of God

Human beings were created in the image of God, with unique abilities.

"Let us make man in our image, according to our likeness." (Genesis 1:26)

This includes the ability to think, love, have a moral sense, and desire relationships.

3. We Were Created to Relate to God

The main purpose of human existence is to have a relationship with God.

"You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart." (Matthew 22:37)

4. We Were Created to Live According to God's Will

Human life has a practical purpose: to live in a way that reflects God's character.

"Fear God and keep His commandments, for this is the whole duty of man." (Ecclesiastes 12:13)

5. We Were Created to Glorify God

The ultimate purpose of life is to honor and reflect God.

"I created them for My glory." (Isaiah 43:7)

6. Existence Points to Eternity

Human life has an eternal dimension.

"God has set eternity in the human heart." (Ecclesiastes 3:11)

Conclusion

According to the Bible, we exist because God created us with purpose. We were created in His image, to relate to Him, live according to His will, and glorify Him.

Human existence has an origin, purpose, and destiny defined by God.

Text based on the Holy Bible.
